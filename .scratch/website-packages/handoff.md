# Concise handoff format

Use these five parts in the Client Language. Keep supporting notes short; retain
all necessary website copy regardless of length. The application's final
submission adds the selected concept image, Client preferences and exact style
confirmation; the earlier document must not claim a completed style choice.

| Part | Deutsch | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Fakten & Ziel:** bestätigtes Angebot/Zielgruppe, öffentliche Angaben, Hauptaktion und genaues Ziel. | **Facts & goal:** confirmed offer/audience, public details, main action and exact destination. |
| 2 | **Website-Inhalte:** Start: gelieferter Wortlaut, klar benannte Lücken und SEO-Vorschlag. Complete: vollständiger faktengestützter Entwurf samt SEO; spätere Rechercheänderungen vor Launch freigeben. | **Website content:** Start: supplied wording, explicit gaps and suggested search metadata. Complete: complete fact-based draft and search metadata; later research changes need approval before launch. |
| 3 | **Gestaltung:** echte Markenregeln, Vermeidungswünsche und delegierte Entscheidungen; Stilwahl folgt in der Anwendung. | **Design:** genuine brand rules, things to avoid and delegated decisions; image selection follows in the application. |
| 4 | **Gebucht:** Paket, Creator-ausgewählte Extras, Website-Korrekturrunden und relevante Ausschlüsse. Keine erneute Kaufverhandlung. | **Purchased scope:** package, Creator-selected extras, website correction rounds and relevant exclusions. No repeated purchase negotiation. |
| 5 | **Noch zu erledigen:** Aufgabe, Verantwortlicher, Status/Lieferweg und nötige Freigabe. Start-Text-/Bildvorbereitung beim Client; Complete-Recherche und Bildarbeit beim Creator. Dateien/Rechtstexte separat; Technikcheck durch Creator. | **Next work:** task, owner, status/delivery route and required approval. Start copy/image preparation belongs to Client; Complete research/image work to Creator. Separate assets/legal delivery; Creator technical checks. |

Status distinctions: **Client-confirmed fact**, **draft/suggestion**, **supplied
evidence**, **Creator work pending**, **Client delivery pending**. Do not describe
unperformed research, unread URLs/images or unpaid licences as verified. Missing
finished Start copy is an explicit preparation dependency, not free Complete
writing. A submitted briefing is not publication approval.
