# Confirm visual style before Project Submission

Clients who are unsure about style should see meaningfully different Visual
Concepts before any tentative design recommendation becomes binding. Selecting
one image is insufficient: collect what to keep and what to leave out or adjust,
show the selected basis and exact preferences, and require explicit confirmation.
Record the summary and server time in the immutable Project Submission.

No concept fitting means no style agreement. Keep another batch available even
after a tentative selection; allow annotated references with the same preferences
and confirmation requirement. Creator scope remains configurable Instructions.
Stored historical submissions remain readable, while every new visual submission
must satisfy the new requirements. Document-only Interviews remain unchanged.

Evaluate three fictional clients without palette, typography or layout choices.
Use real interview/document/concept prompt builders and the current provider
models, render the concepts, and let image-capable Client simulations compare
the actual pixels. Replay their choices through the real React component and
Convex mutations. Include refusal, changing a tentative choice, and references.
Fixtures/harnesses stay in .scratch; generated output stays in ignored runtime.
No hosted mutations, deployments, notification emails or actual pass consumption.

## Outcome

Implemented and evaluated locally on October 13, 2026. Three live simulations
compared nine rendered images and explicitly confirmed preferences; three
offline UI/Convex replays preserved the exact reviewed summaries. The evaluation
also found and repaired a discovery/concept-readiness deadlock. See
[results](results.md) for evidence, limitations and repeat instructions.
